Who We Are

About South Bay Junk Removal

South Bay Junk Removal is a family-owned, local junk removal service located in Torrance and serving customers throughout Redondo Beach, Palos Verdes, the South Bay, Los Angeles, Long Beach, Orange County, and surrounding communities. As a local, family-owned crew, we make it easy to clear unwanted items without lifting, hauling, or transporting them yourself.

From furniture removal, appliance removal, and mattress removal to garage cleanouts, construction debris, yard waste, office junk, pianos, move-outs, and estate cleanouts, our crew handles the work from start to finish. We come to your home, garage, yard, office, or other accessible area, load everything, sort items for recycling or donation when possible, and clean the space after removal.

Our process is fast, convenient, and customer-focused. Text photos and details to 424-360-6135 for a no-pressure estimate, often within about 15 minutes, or request a quote online. With same-day and next-day availability seven days a week, upfront truck-space pricing, and a commitment to responsible disposal, South Bay Junk Removal delivers dependable service wherever you need us.
